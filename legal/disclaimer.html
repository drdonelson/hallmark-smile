Smile Simulator

Medical & AI Disclaimer

Effective date: June 13, 2026

The Smile Simulator creates an AI-generated cosmetic visualization — an artistic illustration of how a smile might look after cosmetic dental work. Please read this before relying on any image it produces.

Not a medical device. Not a diagnosis. Not a guarantee.

What the visualization is

  • An artistic rendering generated by artificial intelligence for cosmetic and educational illustration only.
  • A way to start a conversation with a dentist about cosmetic possibilities.

What it is not

  • It is not a medical or dental diagnosis, treatment plan, or clinical recommendation.
  • It does not predict or guarantee any actual treatment result.
  • It is not a substitute for an in-person examination by a licensed dental professional.

Results vary

Actual results depend on your individual oral anatomy, dental health, the treatment chosen, and the technique and judgment of your treating clinician. Two people who see a similar visualization may have very different real-world options and outcomes. Only a licensed dentist can evaluate what is appropriate and achievable for you.

Please consult a dentist

Before making any decision about dental treatment, consult a licensed dentist who can examine you in person. If you have a dental or medical emergency, contact a qualified professional immediately.
