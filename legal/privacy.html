Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 13, 2026 · Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BD America LLC d/b/a Lucid ROI ("Lucid ROI," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Smile Simulator (the "Service") — an AI tool that produces a cosmetic visualization of a person's smile from a photo they provide. It applies to the Service wherever it is embedded, including on dental-practice websites and on lucidroi.com.

The short version. Your photo is used to generate a smile visualization and is then deleted from our processing systems within minutes. Face-shape detection happens on your own device — not on our servers. We never sell your information, and we never use your photo to train AI models without your separate written permission. If you ask us to email your result to you and to the dental practice, we do that only with your explicit consent.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to provide the Service:

  • The photo you upload. A facial photograph you choose to submit for visualization.
  • Image-derived data. Technical data computed from your photo to create the visualization (e.g., the mouth region, alignment and blending masks, color/lighting values). Facial-landmark detection runs in your browser on your device; the underlying landmark geometry is not transmitted to or stored by us.
  • Contact details you enter (optional, only if you fill in the form): first name, last name, email address, phone number, and your stated area of interest.
  • Consent records. The fact, time, and version of the consent you provide, kept as a compliance record.
  • Basic usage and device data needed to operate and protect the Service (e.g., request counts, error logs, and an IP-derived rate-limit signal to prevent abuse).

2. How we use it

  • To generate your cosmetic smile visualization and, if you request it, a short shareable video.
  • To email your before/after result to you and/or to the dental practice for review and evaluation — only when you have given explicit consent.
  • To pass your contact details to the dental practice you engaged with, so they can follow up about a consultation.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the reliability of the Service (including detecting abuse and preventing misuse).

No model training without your consent. We do not use your photo, your result, or your contact information to train, fine-tune, or improve generalized AI models, and we do not use them for our own marketing, unless you opt in through a separate written agreement.

3. AI processing & your photo

To create the visualization, your photo (and only the image — not your name, email, or phone number) is sent to third-party AI image services that generate the new smile. Your photo is transmitted over an encrypted connection, held only transiently by us as part of processing, and removed from our processing storage automatically within minutes. We instruct and configure these providers to process the image only to return a result, not to retain it for their own purposes or to train their models on it.

4. Facial geometry & biometric notice

The Service locates the mouth and teeth in your photo so it knows where to draw the new smile. This face-shape detection is performed locally in your web browser; the resulting landmark geometry stays on your device and is not sent to us. We do not:

  • create or store a biometric template that could be used to identify you;
  • perform facial recognition or attempt to identify who you are; or
  • sell or share biometric identifiers.

Where any applicable law treats a facial photograph or facial geometry as biometric information, we rely on the consent you provide before processing and retain any such data only for as long as needed to produce your visualization, then delete it as described below.

5. Sharing & email delivery

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With the dental practice you engaged with: your contact details and, if you consent, your before/after visualization, so they can review it and follow up with you.
  • With you: a copy of your before/after result by email, if you ask us to send it.
  • With service providers who help us run the Service (see below), under contracts limiting their use of the data.
  • For legal reasons: to comply with law or valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
  • In a business transfer: as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

7. Retention & deletion

  • Processing images: deleted automatically within minutes of generating your result.
  • Saved results you asked us to email/host: retained for up to 30 days to allow you and the practice to view them, then automatically deleted.
  • Contact details & consent records: retained by the dental practice per their own policies, and by us only as needed to operate the Service and meet legal obligations.

You may request earlier deletion at any time (see Contact).

8. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, non-public/non-guessable media URLs, and short data-retention windows. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and to delete it promptly when it is no longer needed.

9. Dental practices & HIPAA

When you use the Service as a member of the public — for example, uploading your own photo through a website — that activity is generally not regulated by HIPAA. HIPAA applies to protected health information held by a covered health-care provider in connection with treatment.

When a dental practice uses the Service with its own patients in a way that involves protected health information, the practice is the "covered entity" responsible for HIPAA compliance, and Lucid ROI acts as a "business associate." Lucid ROI will execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with practices that require one. In that arrangement we process patient information only to provide the Service and apply safeguards appropriate to the data. Practices may request a BAA at privacy@lucidroi.com.

10. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the CCPA/CPRA or similar state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of certain uses. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any right, contact us or the dental practice that created your visualization.

11. Children & minors

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. A parent or legal guardian must provide consent before any photo of a minor is uploaded, or such photos should not be uploaded.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, communicated through the Service.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@lucidroi.com. BD America LLC d/b/a Lucid ROI, Tennessee. If a dental practice created your visualization, you may also contact that practice directly.
