Automatic follow-up sequence

Every lead that hasn't booked gets this sequence automatically. It stops the moment you change their status from "New" in the Leads tab, and every email includes an unsubscribe link. Emails are branded with your logo, colors, and practice name from the Branding tab; the booking button uses your Booking URL.

Timing: you're notified after 1 hour if a lead is uncontacted · patient reminders go out on day 1, day 3, and day 7.
