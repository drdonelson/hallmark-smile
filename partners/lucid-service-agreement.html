Lucid ROI Service Agreement

This Agreement is between BD America LLC d/b/a Lucid ROI ("Lucid ROI") and the practice identified below (the "Practice"), effective on the date of last signature or electronic acceptance.

1 · Order Form

Full-Service Marketing — as quoted AI Smile Simulator — included add-on

Where this Agreement is accepted electronically through the Lucid ROI activation page, the services, monthly fee, and any promotional trial period shown on that page (the "Activation Summary") form part of this Agreement.

2 · The Services

Lucid ROI will provide the marketing services described in the Activation Summary, which may include: paid advertising management (e.g., Google Ads), search engine optimization, website management, social media content, reputation management, online booking integration, a practice dashboard with lead tracking and reporting, and the AI Smile Simulator (hosted, white-labeled simulator page and website widget with lead capture and consent logging). Lucid ROI may perform services directly or through qualified delivery partners under its direction; Lucid ROI remains responsible to the Practice for the services.

3 · Fees & Billing

  • The monthly fee shown in the Activation Summary is billed in advance to the card or bank account on file. Any promotional free period begins on the go-live date; the first charge occurs when that period ends.
  • No setup fee. No minimum term. The Practice may cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refunds for partial months.
  • Third-party advertising spend (e.g., amounts paid to Google or Meta for ad placement) is not included in the monthly fee unless the Activation Summary states otherwise, and is paid by the Practice directly to the platform.
  • If payment fails and is not resolved within 7 days, services may be suspended until the balance is settled.
  • Pricing is fixed for 12 months from acceptance; thereafter changes require 30 days' written notice.

4 · Practice Responsibilities

  • Provide timely access to the accounts and assets needed to deliver the services (website, domain, ad accounts, brand assets, business listings) and reasonable turnaround on approvals.
  • Use the Smile Simulator only for cosmetic visualization and marketing purposes — not for diagnosis, treatment planning, or clinical decisions. The Service captures explicit patient consent before each simulation.
  • As a healthcare provider, the Practice remains responsible for its own regulatory compliance. Lucid ROI will execute a Business Associate Agreement upon written request.
  • All marketing content published for the Practice is subject to the Practice's approval, and must be truthful and comply with applicable dental board and advertising rules.

5 · Data

  • Leads, patient contact information, ad accounts, and content produced for the Practice belong to the Practice.
  • Simulator patient photos are automatically deleted within 30 days; processing images are deleted within minutes of generation. Consent records are retained for one year as compliance documentation.
  • Lucid ROI does not sell personal information and does not use Practice or patient content to train AI models.

6 · No Guaranteed Outcomes

Marketing performance depends on factors outside either party's control. Lucid ROI provides reporting that ties leads and booked production to their marketing source, but does not guarantee any specific volume of leads, patients, rankings, or revenue.
