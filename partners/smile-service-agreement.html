2 · The Service

Lucid ROI will provide the AI Smile Simulator as a hosted, white-labeled service: a branded simulator page and call-to-action widget installed on the Practice's website, a lead dashboard with before/after photos and status tracking, lead email notifications, patient consent capture with audit logging, and hosted legal documentation. Installation on the Practice's website is included and coordinated with the Practice's website provider.

3 · Fees & Billing

  • The monthly fee for the selected plan is billed in advance to the card or bank account on file, starting on the go-live date.
  • No setup fee. No minimum term. The Practice may cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refunds for partial months.
  • Unused monthly simulations roll over, up to a maximum of one month's plan allowance held in reserve.
  • If payment fails and is not resolved within 7 days, the Service may be suspended until the balance is settled.
  • Pricing is fixed for 12 months from signature; thereafter changes require 30 days' written notice.

4 · Practice Responsibilities

  • The Practice will use the Service only for cosmetic visualization and marketing purposes — not for diagnosis, treatment planning, or clinical decisions.
  • Patient photos are submitted with the patient's knowledge and consent; the Service captures explicit patient consent before each simulation.
  • As a healthcare provider, the Practice remains responsible for its own regulatory compliance. Lucid ROI will execute a Business Associate Agreement upon written request.
  • Marketing use of simulator outputs must be truthful and comply with applicable dental board and advertising rules.

5 · Data

  • Leads and patient contact information collected through the Practice's deployment belong to the Practice.
  • Patient photos are automatically deleted within 30 days; processing images are deleted within minutes of generation.
  • Consent records are retained for one year as compliance documentation.
  • Lucid ROI does not sell personal information and does not use Practice or patient content to train AI models.
